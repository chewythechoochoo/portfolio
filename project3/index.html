Where does our emissions choice matter most?

How much warmer each place gets than it was in 1995–2014, under a low- and a high-emissions future. climate models, 20-year averages.

The vertical axis stays fixed, so places can be compared. Click the chart to change the period.

Data: CMIP6 monthly near-surface air temperature (tas) from the Google Cloud public archive, regridded to 2.5°. Each model is compared with its own 1995–2014 average, then models are averaged with equal weight. Shaded bands show the 10th to 90th percentile across models, not a probability.
